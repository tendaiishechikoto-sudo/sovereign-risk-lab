Football Export Footprint Index (FEFI)

Single-component index Normalized 0–100 measure of total CIES-tracked expatriate players, 2020–2025. This is an export-volume measure, not a rounded "footprint" score — two originally-planned components (value capture, destination concentration) were dropped after source verification found no comparable country-level data.
