Currency Stability & Sovereign Risk Index (CSRI)

Composite score, 0–100, higher = more stable. Weighted blend of inflation, FX spread, reserve adequacy, external debt/GDP, policy-rate volatility, and IMF program status. See methodology for weights and how missing components are handled.
